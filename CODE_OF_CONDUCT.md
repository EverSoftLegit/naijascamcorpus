# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, evidence-led community. Participation is open regardless of nationality, race, ethnicity, gender, disability, age, religion, language, education, professional background, or level of technical experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and curious.
- Critique claims and methods, not people's identities.
- Make room for regional and linguistic differences.
- Credit sources and contributors.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes.
- Protect victims and vulnerable communities.
- Disclose conflicts of interest.
- Keep discussions focused on defensive research and public benefit.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, intimidation, or sustained personal attacks.
- Doxxing, stalking, or publishing private information.
- Victim-blaming, ridicule, or exploiting people's experiences.
- Publishing unverified accusations about identifiable people or organisations.
- Uploading victim evidence, credentials, financial data, or private communications.
- Using the project to promote scams, sell victim data, advertise abusive services, or facilitate fraud.
- Plagiarism, deliberate data poisoning, or misrepresentation of sources.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or remove contributors for serious or repeated violations. Safety-related content may be hidden while reviewed. Decisions should be proportionate, documented, and handled by people without a conflict of interest.

To report conduct concerns, use the private contact route listed in the repository profile once configured. Do not include sensitive victim data in a public issue.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from common open-source community practice. The project will name and publish its chosen enforcement contact before opening broad public participation.
