# Dataset Release Checklist

A release cannot proceed unless all mandatory items are checked.

## Scope and provenance
- [ ] Dataset purpose and intended users are explicit.
- [ ] Every record validates against the current schema.
- [ ] Every record has a source basis and a confidence value.
- [ ] Synthetic records are visibly marked synthetic.
- [ ] Third-party material is not republished without a compatible licence/permission.
- [ ] Country and language coverage claims are supported and appropriately qualified.

## Privacy and safety
- [ ] No direct identifiers, credentials, financial identifiers, private conversations, or raw victim evidence.
- [ ] Re-identification risk considered, including combinations of quasi-identifiers.
- [ ] No live clickable malicious infrastructure or operational abuse instructions.
- [ ] No unsupported allegations against identifiable people or organisations.
- [ ] Sensitive and vulnerable-group implications reviewed.
- [ ] Correction and withdrawal process available.

## Quality
- [ ] At least two independent reviewers approved the release.
- [ ] At least one reviewer completed safety/privacy review.
- [ ] Taxonomy definitions and annotation handbook are versioned.
- [ ] Dataset card includes collection/curation, exclusions, limitations, and intended uses.
- [ ] Validation and reproducibility checks passed.
- [ ] Duplicate and leakage checks completed where relevant.

## Release operations
- [ ] Dataset version and release date assigned.
- [ ] Licence and citation instructions are clear.
- [ ] Checksums generated for release artifacts.
- [ ] Change log and correction notes prepared.
- [ ] Release approval recorded in a decision log.
