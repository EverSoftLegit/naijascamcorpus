# Roadmap

The roadmap is outcome-based. Dates are planning targets and may change based on review capacity and contributor availability.

## Phase 0 — Foundation (Weeks 1–2)

- [x] Draft founding charter and governance model.
- [x] Define safety boundary and synthetic-only launch posture.
- [x] Define initial data schema and taxonomy.
- [ ] Confirm public name and repository slug.
- [ ] Recruit 5–7 founding contributors across at least three African regions and multiple disciplines.
- [ ] Configure GitHub branch protection, private security reporting, discussions, and CODEOWNERS.

**Exit criteria:** policy and review roles are understood; no real-world incident intake is enabled.

## Phase 1 — Public design review (Weeks 3–5)

- [ ] Run a public review of the taxonomy and annotation handbook.
- [ ] Test language-tag and code-switching conventions with reviewers.
- [ ] Validate schema against synthetic edge cases.
- [ ] Publish a landscape review and explicit non-goals.
- [ ] Create a transparent decision log.

**Exit criteria:** taxonomy v0.1 and schema v0.1 accepted by two independent reviewers.

## Phase 2 — Synthetic pilot (Weeks 6–8)

- [ ] Build 25–50 clearly synthetic examples across channels and scam families.
- [ ] Ensure examples cover at least three African regions without implying national representativeness.
- [ ] Create dataset card and benchmark methodology.
- [ ] Test contributor workflow with non-programmers.
- [ ] Publish first tagged pre-release.

**Exit criteria:** all records validate; every record is synthetic, licensed, and reviewed.

## Phase 3 — Research partnerships and governance maturity (Weeks 9–12)

- [ ] Invite universities, language researchers, civil-society groups, and responsible security practitioners to review methods.
- [ ] Establish regional/language working groups.
- [ ] Create a private correction/takedown contact and procedure.
- [ ] Obtain jurisdiction-specific legal and privacy review for any proposed real-world source categories.
- [ ] Decide whether a formal legal host or fiscal sponsor is necessary.

**Exit criteria:** Steering Council approves or rejects the proposal to admit any non-synthetic records. No real-world data is added by default.

## Later releases

- Record-level provenance improvements.
- Public evaluation benchmarks and baseline models.
- Translation quality and language coverage reports.
- Data cards and model cards for derived models.
- Research partnerships and reproducible annual reports.

## Success measures

Measure quality, trust, safety, and reuse rather than raw record volume:

- accepted contribution rate and median review time;
- proportion of records with complete provenance and licence metadata;
- number of active contributors by region and contribution type;
- language and region coverage with explicit gaps;
- correction/withdrawal rate and reasons;
- privacy and safety incidents (target: zero);
- external research, educational, or defensive projects citing a released version.
