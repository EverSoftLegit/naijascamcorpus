# Dataset and Content Licensing

NaijaScamCorpus separates software licensing from dataset licensing because the rights and risks are different.

## Project code

Code in this repository is licensed under the MIT License in `LICENSE` unless a file says otherwise.

## Project-authored documentation and taxonomy

Original prose, annotation guidance, taxonomies, and project-authored metadata are intended to be licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**, unless a specific file or release states otherwise.

## Dataset records

A record must state its own licence. The initial synthetic records are released under CC BY 4.0. A future dataset release may contain records with different rights or may omit records that cannot safely be redistributed.

**Do not assume that the entire corpus is CC BY 4.0.** The licence in the dataset card and record metadata governs the material concerned.

## Third-party material

- A public webpage, article, social post, message, or report is not automatically reusable.
- Prefer links/citations and original summaries over copying source text.
- Do not import third-party datasets unless their licence permits the intended use and the source provenance is documented.
- Preserve required attribution and notices.
- Do not mix incompatible licences without review.
- If rights are uncertain, do not redistribute the material.

## Contributor rights

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you created it or have the necessary rights and permissions to contribute it under the stated licence. You agree to the repository's DCO sign-off process. Do not submit confidential, employer-owned, customer-owned, or third-party material without authority.

## Data release citation

Each published dataset version will include a dataset card, version, release date, schema version, record count, source methodology, geographic/language coverage, exclusions, known limitations, licence, and citation instructions.

## Not legal advice

This policy is an operational project rule, not a legal opinion on copyright, database rights, privacy, or cross-border data transfers.
